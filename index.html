      Chapter 01

      About

      Where I come from and how I work.

      Get to know me

      From wings and laminates to data.

      I studied Aeronautics and Astronautics at Institut Teknologi Bandung, with a bachelor's thesis on the finite element analysis of a composite structure.

      After that I taught physics, built carbon fibre and fibreglass parts by hand, and started two community projects in Serang. Since 2024 I have lived in Linz, where I study for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Johannes Kepler University.

      How I work

      Three habits behind every project.

      1. Build it from scratch first.A Vision Transformer, a DQN with its own environment, two-view 3D reconstruction. Writing the parts myself shows me what a library hides.
      2. Check it against something trusted.A simulation is compared with the full solver. A model is judged on held-out data, and on where it looks.
      3. Teach what I learn.Tutoring, Rumah Pelajar, SantriLab, GORGA. Explaining a thing is the fastest way to find what I still don't understand.

      Chapter 02

      Research
      & ML

      One thesis, five models built from scratch.

      Master's thesis · JKU Linz

      Record a flow once, replay it cheaply.

      Recurrence CFD stores a simulated flow and replays it to move heat at a fraction of the cost. I write the models as C functions for Ansys Fluent and check each against the full solver. Supervised by Prof. Stefan Pirker.

      0.008 %replay vs. reference
      35 → 81 %cooling recovered
      Thesis summary ↗ Benchmark repo ↗

      Computer vision

      A Vision Transformer that shows where it looks.

      Patch embedding, self-attention and training loop written from scratch in PyTorch. Validation accuracy is 99.8 %, but the dataset is easy. The useful part is attention rollout: the heat map falls on the crack.

      Repository ↗

      Segmentation

      Crack masks with YOLOv8-seg.

      Instance segmentation for surface cracks, trained on the public crack-seg dataset (4,029 images). The overlays are predictions on held-out test images.

      Repository ↗

      Video

      Detect, track, and follow the motion.

      YOLOv8/v10 detection, ByteTrack to keep identities over time, and dense and sparse optical flow. Optical flow is a velocity field for pixels, which is why I like it.

      Repository ↗

      3D vision

      From two photos to a point cloud.

      SIFT matching, the essential matrix, pose recovery and triangulation, step by step. On a synthetic cube the recovered pose error is 0°. A multi-view COLMAP reconstruction of a real building sits next to it.

      Repository ↗

      Reinforcement learning

      A Deep Q-Network, environment included.

      DQN with replay buffer and target network in PyTorch, on a CartPole environment written without gym. Greedy evaluation reaches the 500-step limit after about 150 episodes.

      Repository ↗
